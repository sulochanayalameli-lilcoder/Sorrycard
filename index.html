Hey Anurag,

I am genuinely sorry for hurting you and for making you feel that I wasn't happy for you.

I never wanted you to feel that way. But I also understand that saying I didn't intend to hurt you doesn't erase how my actions made you feel.

Your happiness genuinely matters to me. I have always seen you as someone I can look up to and learn from. I admire you, and I am proud of the things you achieve.

You're not just another friend to me. You're one of the people I truly value in my life. I don't want you to question whether I support you or whether I want good things for you.

If I made you feel that I wasn't celebrating your happiness, I am sorry. You deserved to feel supported, not misunderstood.

I cannot change what happened, but I can acknowledge it, learn from it and be more thoughtful about how I express myself.

I am not asking you to pretend that nothing happened. You can take your time. I just hope that one day you'll believe that I genuinely want to see you happy and successful.

Thank you for being someone who inspires me. I hope our friendship gets past this, because it really does mean a lot to me.

I'm truly sorry, Anurag. 🤍

Always wishing you the best,
